## 📑 What's Inside the Pitch Deck

The pitch deck slides (`Presentation.pdf` / `Slides`) cover:
1. **The Core Problem**:
   - Over 250 million students across India learn in regional Indic mediums.
   - Quality STEM educators in vernacular languages are severely scarce.
   - LLMs suffer from mathematical hallucinations when teaching algebra and calculus.
2. **Our Solution**:
   - Voice-first Socratic AI in 10 Indic languages via Gnani Timbre & Prisma.
   - Computer Algebra System (SymPy CAS) acting as a deterministic mathematical referee.
   - Multimodal whiteboard canvas with normalized coordinate strokes & PDF worksheet ingestion.
3. **Market Opportunity & Social Impact**:
   - Addressable market of Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural schools across India.
   - Integration with state curriculum textbooks (NCERT & State Boards).
4. **Technical Innovations & Differentiators**:
   - Zero hallucination via formal CAS equivalence verification.
   - Dual-loop audio pipeline: browser 16kHz mono PCM WAV encoding.
   - PWA mobile responsiveness with full touch and stylus ergonomics.
5. **Business Model & Roadmap**:
   - B2G and B2B school district licensing.
   - Offline-first on-device edge model deployments.

## 🎥 What's Featured in the Video Demonstration

The 3-minute video walkthrough demonstrates:
1. **Indic Voice Interaction**:
   - Asking math and science questions verbally in Kannada / Hindi.
   - Real-time speech transcription (Gnani Prisma STT) and low-latency audio response (Gnani Timbre TTS).
2. **Interactive Whiteboard & Socratic Guidance**:
   - Writing an algebraic equation on the digital canvas ($2x = 8$).
   - Gemini Live multimodal perception of handwritten strokes.
3. **Deterministic Math Verification in Action**:
   - Student intentional mistake ($x = 8$ instead of $x = 4$).
   - SymPy CAS instantly flags red **`incorrect`** badge and diagnoses the exact operational error without revealing the answer.
   - Corrected step ($x = 4$) verified with green **`correct`** badge.
4. **PDF Textbook Worksheet Ingestion**:
   - Loading sample math worksheet PDF directly into the canvas.
   - AI vision inspecting worksheet questions and providing guided step-by-step assistance.
